Privacy Policy — Magix Puzzle

Last updated: 19 Nov 2026. This policy explains how Magix Games ("we") handles information when you play Magix Puzzle ("the game").

1. Information stored on your device

The game saves your progress, best score, language and sound settings, and your privacy choices in your device's local storage. This data stays on your device and is not sent to our servers.

2. Advertising

The game shows ads provided by Google (AdMob / AdSense). Google and its partners may collect and use your device's advertising identifier, IP address and related technical data to deliver, measure and (if you allow it) personalize ads. See Google's advertising policies.

  • Personalized ads are off by default. You can turn them on or off any time in Settings → Privacy.
  • On iOS you may also be asked for permission to track; you can change this in your device settings.
  • Where required (for example the EEA and UK), you will be shown a consent form before personalized ads are served.

3. Information we do not collect

We do not ask for your name, e-mail address, contacts, precise location, photos or microphone access.

3b. Online ranking and group challenges (optional)

If you open the ranking or play together with friends, you choose a nickname. We then store on our server: your nickname, your best score, your level, a random player ID generated on your device, and (for group challenges) the room code and your progress in that room. We do not collect your real name, e-mail or location for this feature. Do not use your real name as a nickname. You can stop at any time and ask us to delete your ranking data using the contact in section 8; send us your player ID and nickname.

3c. Anonymous usage statistics

To improve the game we use Google Analytics for Firebase to collect anonymous usage events (for example levels started and completed, helpers used and ads shown) together with technical data such as device model, OS version, country and a random app-instance ID. We do not link this data to your name or contact details. You can turn it off at any time in Settings → Privacy → Usage statistics.

3d. In-app purchases

Purchases are processed by Google Play; we never see your card or payment details. To confirm purchases and restore them on a new device we use RevenueCat, which receives a random app user ID and the purchase receipt from Google Play. We do not receive your name or e-mail address through purchases.

4. Internet connection

The game requires an internet connection to display ads and to check your connection status.

5. Children

The game is intended for a general audience and is not directed to children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us and we will help remove it.

6. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete or restrict the use of your data, to withdraw consent at any time, and to complain to a data-protection authority. You can withdraw consent by changing the settings in the game or by clearing the game's data.

7. Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date above and ask for your agreement again when the changes are significant.
